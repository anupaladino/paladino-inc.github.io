A revitalised Metcha probably wouldn't work.
You may be right.

The Metcha we built between 2018 and 2022 belonged to a very different media environment, operating model and cost structure. The opportunity today is not to bring that Metcha back.

It is to ask: what could Metcha become now?

02 / 12
The idea is still relevant
The premise was never simply to talk about leather.

Metcha was created to bring people closer to leather through the things they already cared about: fashion, design, sneakers, interiors, objects, culture and creativity. Leather was not necessarily the subject. It was the material connecting the stories.

That idea may be even more relevant today than it was then.

03 / 12
What changed since 2022
Social media became interest media.

People increasingly discover content through interests rather than only through the people and brands they follow. This creates an important opportunity for leather: we no longer need people to be interested in leather first. We can reach them through the culture surrounding it.

04 / 12
The old model
Built around production. Ambitious by design.
  • Original editorial production
  • Shoots and commissioned content
  • A relatively large production ecosystem
  • Paid distribution
  • Constant audience acquisition

It created remarkable work. It was also expensive and operationally demanding.

~US$1.5M / year
05 / 12
What the new Metcha could be, I
Production→Curation

The world is already producing the stories. Every day, designers, brands, studios, makers, architects and creators are doing extraordinary things with leather. The role of Metcha could be to find them first.

Cool huntingTrend spottingDesigner discovery Material innovation, objects, craftEmerging aesthetics, new applications
06 / 12
What the new Metcha could be, II
Audience→Participation

Designers contribute. Brands collaborate. Schools participate. Tanneries reveal innovation. Creators interpret stories. The industry does not simply fund Metcha, the industry and the creative community help feed it.

Paid reach→Earned relevance

Mentions. Shares. Reposts. Creator participation. Brand collaborations. Editorial pickup. Search presence. Organic discovery. Less buying attention. More earning it.

The new operating system
Old Metcha: Create → Publish → Promote → Acquire
New Metcha: Discover → Curate → Connect → Amplify

Curation

Channels
Distribution across the platforms where design culture actually lives.
Community

And across all four: earned cultural presence.
